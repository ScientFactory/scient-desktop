# WhatsApp

Link your WhatsApp account to Scient as a device so your agents can work with
your messages. The link runs on the computer running the Scient environment you
choose in **Tools on**.

## Link WhatsApp

1. Open **Settings → Extensions → Discover** and choose **WhatsApp**.
2. Choose **Link WhatsApp**. Read the note about risks, then choose **Link
   WhatsApp** again to see the code.
3. On your phone, open **WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device**.
   Scan the code on your screen. The computer appears under its own name in
   **Linked devices**.

## Know the risks

Linking apps like Scient is unofficial. WhatsApp has sometimes restricted or
banned accounts linked this way. The risk is higher in these cases.

- Scient runs on a server or in the cloud.
- The phone number is new or virtual, or belongs to a verified business account.
- Many messages go out quickly, or to people who have never messaged you.

Scient spaces out messages, never sends in bulk, and sends only when you ask an
agent to. If WhatsApp gives an end time for a restriction, Scient shows it and
stays disconnected until then.

## What agents can do

Agents can check whether WhatsApp is connected, list chats, read a chat, search
messages, find contacts, and send one text message to one chat. Send the first
message to someone new from your phone.

## Stay linked or unlink

Link once. Scient reconnects after a restart. The link can end if you remove the
device from your phone, the phone stays offline for about 14 days, WhatsApp bans
the account, you move the account to a new phone, or you unlink in Scient.

Scient copies chats to its data folder on the computer running the link. Choose
**Unlink** on the WhatsApp page and confirm to log out that device and delete
Scient's copy of your chats.

## WhatsApp Business

On the same page, **WhatsApp Business → Open** takes you to Meta's tools for
business setup, message templates, and sending. Those tools can't read chats.

## Features still unavailable
