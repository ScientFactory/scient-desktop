# Invite people to a thread

A thread can have people in it as well as the agent. People you invite watch the
thread live in their browser, talk with you there, and, if you allow it, talk to the
agent. It works on a computer with Scient Connect on and reachable.

## Invite someone

1. Choose **Share → Invite people…** in the thread header, or **Invite people…** in
   the thread's menu or the command palette.
2. Pick what the person may do and for how long (1, 7 or 30 days), then **Create
   invite** and send them the link. Each link works for one person; create another
   for each person.
3. They open the link, type their name, and see the thread as you do, including
   command output and the agent's reasoning, from the start of the thread.

Everyone in the room sees the same conversation. What differs is each person's
permission:

- **Can watch** sees the thread live and cannot write.
- **Can talk with people** also writes to people in the thread and can suggest a
  message for the agent.
- **Can also message the agent** also sends messages to the agent, sends anyone's
  message to it, and stops it. The agent runs on your computer with your files and
  accounts, so give this only to people you trust with that.

The header shows who is in the room and who has it open. Open it to change a permission,
copy an invite again, remove someone, or **Stop sharing**. A change applies at once.
Archiving the thread also ends the room. Removing someone stops their access; it
cannot take back what they already saw.

## Talk to people, or to the agent

While a thread has a room, a line above the composer says where your message goes:

- **To the agent**: as usual.
- **To people only**: the agent never sees it. Choose **Post to people instead** to
  switch, or **Reply** on someone's message.

Messages stay in the order they were written. Any message to people has **Send to
agent**: the agent then gets it as a new message at the end, naming who wrote it and
who sent it, and the original shows "Sent to the agent by …" where it was written.
Messages people suggest for the agent say so, and the thread shows in the sidebar as
waiting for you until you open it.

People's messages, and the files they attach, stay in the thread after sharing stops.
They are not part of what the agent remembers unless someone sends them to it, and
copies of the thread (sync, share links, continuing on another computer) leave them
out.

Guest links are a beta, on unless you turn them off: **Settings → Labs → Guest
links** off stops every room on that computer. If Scient says other devices can't
reach the computer, **Settings → Sharing & access** shows why; see
[Scient Connect troubleshooting](./scient-connect-troubleshooting.md).
