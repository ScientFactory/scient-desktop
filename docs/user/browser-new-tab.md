# New browser tab

An empty browser tab starts with **Gmail** and **Apps**, a scrolling list of Google and its apps,
from Drive, Docs and Scholar to Colab, Classroom and Earth. They open in the profile shown in the
toolbar, so you reach that profile's account. If it isn't signed in, Google shows its sign-in
first.

On a profile whose email address is at a university, that university's **Moodle** joins them, or
**Opal** at the Open University. Scient knows the Moodle of the Hebrew University, Tel Aviv,
Technion, Ben-Gurion, Ariel and Reichman. For another university, Moodle appears once the profile
signs in to its Moodle or opens it twice. To have a Moodle on any other profile, add it as a
shortcut.

Below them is a search box that works like the address bar. Type a web address to open it, or
anything else to search with the search engine chosen in Settings. It uses the profile shown in the
toolbar.

Shortcuts sit below the search box, above your local previews and recently used pages. Sites you add
come first, then the sites you open most, then Scholar, PubMed, arXiv, Overleaf, Drive and Calendar.

Press **Add** to add a site with a name of your choice. To change one, hover it, press **⋯** and
choose **Edit** to rename it or change its link, or **Remove**. An edited shortcut becomes one of
yours; a removed one stays away until you add it again. Shortcuts are shared by all your projects
on this device. Sites opened in an Incognito tab aren't counted.
