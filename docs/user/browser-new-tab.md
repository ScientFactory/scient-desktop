# New browser tab

An empty browser tab starts with **Google**, **Gmail**, **Drive** and **Apps**, which holds your
Google Account, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Meet, Tasks, Photos, YouTube, Maps, Translate,
Scholar, Gemini Notebook, Gemini and Colab. They open in the profile shown in the toolbar, so you
reach that profile's account. If it isn't signed in, Google shows its sign-in first.

Shortcuts sit below them, above your local previews and recently used pages. Sites you add come
first, then the sites you open most, then Scholar, PubMed, arXiv, Overleaf and Calendar.

Press **Add** to add a site with a name of your choice. To change one, hover it, press **⋯** and
choose **Edit** to rename it or change its link, or **Remove**. An edited shortcut becomes one of
yours; a removed one stays away until you add it again. Shortcuts are shared by all your projects
on this device. Sites opened in an Incognito tab aren't counted.
