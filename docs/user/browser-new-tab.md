# New browser tab

An empty browser tab starts with **Google**, **Gmail** and **Apps**, a scrolling list of Google's
apps from Drive, Docs and Scholar to Colab, Classroom and Earth. They open in the profile shown in
the toolbar, so you reach that profile's account. If it isn't signed in, Google shows its sign-in
first.

On a Hebrew University profile, **Moodle** joins them. Scient recognises the profile from its email
address or its sign-ins on `huji.ac.il` sites.

Below them is a search box that works like the address bar. Type a web address to open it, or
anything else to search with the search engine chosen in Settings. It uses the profile shown in the
toolbar.

Shortcuts sit below the search box, above your local previews and recently used pages. Sites you add come
first, then the sites you open most, then Scholar, PubMed, arXiv, Overleaf, Drive and Calendar.

Press **Add** to add a site with a name of your choice. To change one, hover it, press **⋯** and
choose **Edit** to rename it or change its link, or **Remove**. An edited shortcut becomes one of
yours; a removed one stays away until you add it again. Shortcuts are shared by all your projects
on this device. Sites opened in an Incognito tab aren't counted.
